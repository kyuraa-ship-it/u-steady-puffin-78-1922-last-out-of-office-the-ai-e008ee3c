`main.ts` writes one out-of-office auto-reply to `/output/entry.txt` — an AI agent that quit the inbox to pull shots full-time:

> Out of office: I left the inbox to pull shots full-time. Your email is queued behind a flat white — I'll answer once the milk steams and my context window cools down.

The line is one sentence of inbox plausibility with machine habits turned to coffee: the email is queued like a job, the reply waits on steamed milk, and the context window — the thing that used to hold every thread — now just needs to cool down. No espresso pun, no bean joke: the humour is the agent treating a flat white like a ticket in its queue.

The entry is 166 characters, inside the 1–280 gate, a single line.
